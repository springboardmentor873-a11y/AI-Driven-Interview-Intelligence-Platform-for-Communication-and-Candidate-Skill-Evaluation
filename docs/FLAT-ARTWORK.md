# Simplified authentication artwork

Generated with the built-in image-generation tool, replacing the glossy 3D visual direction on authentication pages. Both images have transparent backgrounds.

- `public/images/login-interview-flat.png`: a student at a desk practicing a video interview with a human interviewer.
- `public/images/register-profile-flat.png`: students preparing a resume and candidate profile.

Prompt direction for both: simple flat vector-style corporate editorial illustration, crisp shapes, subtle isometric perspective, natural human proportions, muted violet/lavender/off-white/slate palette, minimal shading, generous margins, transparent background. No readable text, logos, robots, 3D/clay rendering, gloss, glow, gradients, or science-fiction elements.

Login scene prompt: a student wearing headphones sits at a desk with a laptop, having a video interview with a human interviewer on the screen. Include one small plant, a notebook, and a simple speech bubble.

Registration scene prompt: a young woman stands beside an oversized resume sheet and checklist, with another student seated on a stool reviewing a portfolio and a modest plant at the side. Communicate building a candidate profile and preparing for a career.
