## Brand & Style

This design system embodies an empathetic, intelligent, and composed environment tailored for high-stakes recruitment scenarios. Traditional technical interview platforms often evoke anxiety through harsh high-contrast dark modes, aggressive red-line error states, and rigid metrics. This system deliberately pivots toward calm reassurance, cognitive clarity, and human dignity.

### Aesthetic Persona
- **Composed & Supportive:** Soft pastel surfaces reduce cognitive fatigue, lowering user stress during simulated interviews and candidate assessments.
- **Intelligent Simplicity:** Minimalist structural layouts emphasize substance over spectacle. Whitespace is generous, structured, and intentional.
- **Warm Authority:** Professional enough for enterprise talent acquisitions and administrators, yet welcoming and encouraging for first-time job seekers and students.

### Stylistic Philosophy
The design language is rooted in refined, soft modernism. It balances delicate tonal surfaces with pristine crisp-white elevation cards, subtle borders, and a grounding deep slate typographic foundation. There are zero neon accents, intense glowing states, or jarring mechanical animations. Transitions are smooth and weighted, evoking quiet confidence.

## Colors

The palette is anchored by calming, nature-derived tones and gentle atmospheric pastels. Deep slate typography maintains a strict 4.5:1+ WCAG AA contrast ratio against all primary, secondary, and tertiary tinted containers.

### Core Swatches
- **Primary Accent (`#6FA89A` - Calm Sage / Seafoam Teal):** The single source of action and progress. Used for key callouts, primary buttons, interactive focus indicators, and successful completion states.
- **Secondary Atmospheric (`#F0EDFA` - Soft Lavender Frost):** Contextual tinted background for secondary modules, assessment feedback panels, and qualitative review cards.
- **Tertiary Accent (`#FBEDE5` - Warm Pastel Peach):** Denotes warnings, tips, preparatory advice, and recruiter alerts without creating alarming danger indicators.
- **Primary Neutral (`#26364A` - Deep Slate Navy):** The primary ink color for high-contrast legibility across all typography and icon paths.
- **Secondary Neutral (`#647A94` - Muted Blue Slate):** For subtitles, metadata, breadcrumbs, helper hints, and table column headers.

### Surfaces and Containers
- **Canvas (`#EAF2FB` - Soft Ice Blue):** Full-bleed base application canvas providing cool, low-strain ambient illumination.
- **Elevated Surfaces (`#FFFFFF` - Pure White):** All primary operational surfaces, cards, modal sheets, and dropdowns.
- **Soft Mint Container (`#E7F3EE` - Mint Mist):** Metric status indicators, skill confidence scorecards, and AI competency highlights.
- **Border (`#E2E8F0` - Subtle Slate):** Structural separator line maintaining tactile delineation between pure white containers and soft pastel canvas backdrops.

## Typography

The typography relies on Plus Jakarta Sans to deliver geometric balance, open letter apertures, and friendly humanized curves. This structure guarantees quick scanning during timed assessments without appearing cold or mechanical.

- **Display & Headlines:** Tightly tracked headings deliver commanding confidence across dashboard views and candidate score overviews.
- **Body:** Open line heights ensure sustained reading comfort for multi-paragraph AI question prompts, behavioral feedback summaries, and candidate resumes.
- **Labels & Micro-copy:** Uppercase or mixed-case semantic chips and meta labels are weighted at `500` or `600` to remain crisp against soft tinted containers.

## Layout & Spacing

This design system uses an adaptive 12-column layout model organized along an 8-point base rhythm, supplemented by 4-point micro-spacing values.

### Grid & Breakpoints
- **Desktop (1280px+):** 12-column fluid grid, 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters, 32px (`2rem`) outer canvas margins. Maximum readable container width maxes out at 1440px to prevent visual detachment across ultra-wide monitors.
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px):** 8-column layout, 20px gutters, 24px margins. Sidebars collapse to icon-only rails or responsive flyout drawers.
- **Mobile (320px - 767px):** 4-column layout, 16px (`1rem`) gutters, 16px (`1rem`) margins. Multi-column metric cards stack into linear progressive reading flows.

### Structural Flow
Content avoids dense enterprise clutter. Top app navigation bars maintain fixed 64px vertical clearance. Card interiors follow a strict 24px (`space-lg`) internal padding rule (`p-6`) for all standard modules, dropping to 16px (`space-md`) on mobile screens to preserve horizontal area.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy relies on clean surface layering, contrast separation, and low-opacity diffused shadows rather than heavy structural borders.

### Elevation Hierarchy
- **Elevation 0 (Canvas Base):** Background `#EAF2FB`. The foundational canvas layer with zero elevation.
- **Elevation 1 (Standard Card & Container):** Background `#FFFFFF` with a 1px border of `#E2E8F0` and `shadow-sm` (`0 1px 3px rgba(38, 54, 74, 0.04), 0 1px 2px rgba(38, 54, 74, 0.02)`). Standard container for questions, video viewports, and candidate profile lists.
- **Elevation 2 (Interactive Floating & Hover):** Triggered on card hover, popovers, and contextual action trays. Features `shadow-md` with calm tinted diffusion (`0 4px 12px -2px rgba(38, 54, 74, 0.06), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(38, 54, 74, 0.03)`).
- **Elevation 3 (Modals, Role Switchers & Sheets):** Elevated overlays utilizing `0 16px 32px -4px rgba(38, 54, 74, 0.08)`. Backdrops use a subtle, semi-opaque wash (`rgba(38, 54, 74, 0.25)`) coupled with a gentle backdrop blur (4px) to retain spatial awareness.

## Shapes

The shape system conveys approachability, safety, and modern precision. Sharp angles are systematically replaced by smooth, generous radii.

- **Primary Cards & Containers:** Standardized at `rounded-2xl` (16px / `1rem` on base scale up to 24px for macro panels), creating soft, organic content framing.
- **Buttons, Form Inputs, & Modals:** Standardized at `rounded-xl` (12px / `0.75rem`), giving interactive touchpoints a pillowed, click-friendly feel.
- **Badges, Tags, & Status Pills:** Full rounded pill format (`rounded-full` / 9999px) to distinctly contrast against rectangular metric cards.
- **Role Indicators:** Subtle 8px (`rounded-md`) squircle geometry to maintain clear administrative distinction without breaking aesthetic flow.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Solid `#6FA89A` with pure `#FFFFFF` text. Border radius `rounded-xl`, padding `10px 20px`, typography `label-md`. Hover state applies a 6% black tint overlay (`#63978A`) with no abrupt translation. Active state subtly scales to 0.98.
- **Secondary Action:** Pure `#FFFFFF` background, 1px border `#E2E8F0`, `#26364A` text. Hover shifts background to `#EAF2FB` with border transitioning to `#6FA89A/40`.
- **Subtle / Ghost:** Transparent background, `#26364A` text. Hover fills with `#EAF2FB` or `#F0EDFA` at 60% opacity.
- **Disabled State:** Background `#E2E8F0`, text `#647A94`, opacity 60%, cursor not-allowed.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Text Inputs & Dropdowns:** Pure `#FFFFFF` background, 1px border `#E2E8F0`, `rounded-xl` geometry, padding `12px 16px`. Text rendered in `#26364A` with placeholders in `#647A94`. Focus state applies border color `#6FA89A` and an atmospheric focus ring: `0 0 0 3px rgba(111, 168, 154, 0.20)`.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Size `20x20px`, border 1.5px `#E2E8F0`, `rounded-md` (checkbox) or `rounded-full` (radio). Selected state fills with `#6FA89A` showcasing a pure white interior checkmark or dot.

### Badges, Chips & Status Pills
Badges utilize a soft-fill styling model paired with high-contrast text:
- **Success / Competency (Soft Mint):** Background `#E7F3EE`, text `#1E5E4E`, border 1px solid `rgba(30, 94, 78, 0.12)`.
- **Insight / Preparatory Note (Soft Peach):** Background `#FBEDE5`, text `#8C4B27`, border 1px solid `rgba(140, 75, 39, 0.12)`.
- **Interview Stage / Role Track (Soft Lavender):** Background `#F0EDFA`, text `#4F3C7E`, border 1px solid `rgba(79, 60, 126, 0.12)`.

### Cards & Modular Containers
- **Standard Card:** Elevation 1, pure `#FFFFFF`, 1px border `#E2E8F0`, `rounded-2xl`, internal padding `24px` (`p-6`). Headers, bodies, and footers separated by spacing rhythm rather than heavy divider rules.
- **Assessment Review Card:** Incorporates alternating surface tints; main card `#FFFFFF`, with nested code responses or rubric feedback housed in `#F0EDFA` or `#EAF2FB` at `rounded-xl`.

### Navigation & Specialized App Chrome
- **Top Navigation Bar:** Height 64px, background `#FFFFFF/90` with subtle backdrop-blur (8px), bottom border 1px solid `#E2E8F0`. Houses global search, notification triggers, and user profile drawer.
- **Collapsible Sidebar:** Background `#FFFFFF`, right border 1px solid `#E2E8F0`. Features persistent role switcher badge tags (`Student`, `Recruiter`, `Administrator`) at the header block with soft lavender and mint accents. Nav items feature an 8px radius with a light `#EAF2FB` background fill on active selection.
- **Video & Mock Interview Screen Container:** Pure `#FFFFFF` wrapper card with custom AI transcription panel on the right (`#EAF2FB` canvas fill) and responsive audio waveform visualizer styled in `#6FA89A`.
