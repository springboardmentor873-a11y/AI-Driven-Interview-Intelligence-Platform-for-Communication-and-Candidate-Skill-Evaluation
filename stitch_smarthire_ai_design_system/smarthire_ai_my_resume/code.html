SmartHire ATS Guidelines

Our AI extraction engine relies on natural linguistic tokens to formulate tailored interview scenarios. Follow these recommendations:

  • Keep margins between 0.5 to 1.0 inches.
  • Use standard system fonts (Arial, Calibri, Helvetica, Plus Jakarta Sans).
  • Never embed essential contact details or skills inside vector headers/footers.
  • Verify your chronological timeline format (e.g., "MM/YYYY - MM/YYYY").
